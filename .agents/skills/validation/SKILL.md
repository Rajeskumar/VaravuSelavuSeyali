Read `.ai-sdlc/lifecycle.md` for scope, risk and phase boundaries, then follow
`.ai-sdlc/workflows/validate.md`. Use the applicable templates and schemas under `.ai-sdlc/`;
work records live under `docs/sdlc/`. Read only the relevant product context and standards.
Preserve user intent and existing authorization. Report the phase, artifacts, evidence and remaining
questions. A role/skill does not grant permission to commit, push, deploy or mutate external systems.

Board tracking (queue/pick up/status mirror) follows `.ai-sdlc/lifecycle.md` "Work tracking"; that standing authorization is the only external write allowed without a new ask.
