This Privacy Policy explains how CerebroOS ("CerebroOS," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use TrackSpense, our expense-tracking application, available on the web at expense.cerebroos.com and as a mobile app (together, the "Service"). By using the Service you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who we are

The Service is operated by CerebroOS, located at CerebroOS HQ. For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at privacy@cerebroos.com.

2. Information we collect

Unsaved expense drafts. To recover interrupted entries, the web app keeps a draft in this browser tab's session storage, and the mobile app uses the device's secure storage. b. Information collected automatically

Optional web analytics uses Google Analytics only when collection is enabled and you allow analytics cookies. It helps us understand page and feature usage. Analytics may process technical metadata such as browser/device information and network IP addresses. We exclude expense amounts, descriptions, receipt contents, group identifiers, names, contact details and AI conversations from analytics events. We do not use your email address as an analytics user identifier.

You can allow or decline optional analytics in Analytics preferences, available on the home-page footer and in Account. Withdrawing permission stops new collection and may reload the page; it cannot recall requests already sent. Analytics choices are separate from AI-feature consent. If your browser cannot save a preference, analytics stays off for the current page; allow site storage to save your choice. Collection remains off on sensitive invitation, password-reset and assistant approval pages. Our infrastructure provider (Cloudflare) may separately process request data to deliver and protect the Service.

The iOS and Android apps can use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, each only if you turn it on. When you first open the app, it asks once whether to allow usage analytics and, separately, crash reports. Both are off unless you allow them, and you can change either at any time in Account → Privacy. Usage analytics records which screens and features are used and whether actions worked. Crash reports record crashes and errors with the app version and what failed. Neither includes expense amounts, descriptions, receipt contents, group names or identifiers, names, email addresses, passwords, sign-in tokens or AI conversations. We do not use your email address or an account identifier for these services, we do not use the advertising identifier on iOS or Android, and we do not use them for advertising.

Google acts as our service provider for these services. With your permission it receives technical information such as the app version, operating system and device model, a device identifier created for the app (not your advertising identifier), and approximate location derived from your IP address. Withdrawing permission stops new collection but cannot recall data already sent. Because this data is not linked to your account, we cannot delete it for one account; it is removed automatically when its retention period ends [CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLISHING: the retention period configured for usage analytics in Google Analytics, and the Crashlytics retention period].

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to: provide, maintain, and improve the Service; record and analyze your expenses; generate insights, charts, and AI-assisted answers; process recurring-expense templates; authenticate you and secure your account; respond to support requests; monitor performance and prevent abuse; and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Artificial intelligence features

Three features send data to a third-party AI service, currently Google's Gemini:

  • Ask (the AI Analyst). Your question and the figures needed to answer it: expense descriptions, merchant names, amounts and dates, budgets, and for groups you belong to, the group name, member display names and balances.
  • Receipt scanning. Receipts are read by our own text recognition first. Only when that read isn't confident is the receipt image or PDF sent to the AI service, and never if you haven't allowed AI features.
  • Category suggestions. The description text of an expense, only when our built-in rules can't categorize it.

We do not send your password, payment card numbers, or access tokens. The first time you use one of these features the app asks whether you allow it; if you decline, nothing is sent and the rest of the app keeps working (receipts are read by our own reader only, and category suggestions use only built-in rules). You can withdraw the choice at any time by clearing the app's stored data (in the mobile app, signing out also clears it), and we may ask again after material changes. Analytics and crash-report choices are separate and are changed in Account → Privacy (see Section 2b). We send only what is needed to answer the request. The provider handles that data under its own terms for the Gemini API [CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLISHING: the provider's retention period and whether data is used to train models for the API plan in use]. Prompts and answers are not stored by TrackSpense beyond the conversation on your screen.

5. How we share information

We share personal information only as follows:

  • Service providers. Vendors who process data on our behalf to run the Service, including hosting (Google Cloud), content delivery/security (Cloudflare), analytics (Google Analytics on the website, only if you accept analytics cookies; Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics in the mobile apps, only if you allow them), the AI service (Google Gemini, only if you allow AI features), and Google sign-in (loaded only when you choose "Continue with Google"). They are permitted to use the data only to provide services to us and must protect it to a standard equivalent to this Policy.
  • Legal & safety. When required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users, the public, or CerebroOS.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share your financial data with advertisers.

Connected assistants (when enabled)

If you connect an external assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT, TrackSpense shares only the personal-expense data allowed by the permissions you select. That assistant and its provider process the shared data under their own policies. Group expenses are excluded. Permission to prepare an expense does not authorize saving it: every addition requires your separate review in TrackSpense. Possible-duplicate details may be included in the review even when broader expense-reading access is not selected.

You can disconnect an assistant in Connected assistants. Signing out everywhere or changing/resetting your password also revokes connections. This stops future access; it cannot remove data already held by an external provider. Connections, approval records and basic activity are included in your account data export and deletion.

6. Data retention

Assistant drafts expire after 24 hours. Cleanup removes full draft/result payloads and warnings 30 days after completion, rejection or expiry; minimal operation identifiers remain to prevent a retry from adding an expense twice. Activity is retained for 180 days and rate-limit windows for two days. Physical removal follows the cleanup schedule, and account deletion removes these user-linked records earlier.

We retain your personal and financial information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. When you delete your account (see Section 8), your profile, personal expenses and receipt items, budgets, recurring templates, tags, cards, sign-ins and invitation records are deleted immediately.

Shared group records stay. Expenses you added to a group, with their descriptions, merchants, notes and amounts, remain so the other members' balances and history stay correct. Your name on those records becomes "Anonymous User" and your email address is removed from them. Copies in system backups age out on the backup schedule of our hosting provider [CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLISHING: backup retention period]. We may also keep certain records where we must to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you as required by law.

8. Your rights and choices

You can:

  • Access and update your profile and expense data within the Service.
  • Export your data from the Profile screen: "Export all expenses (CSV)" or "Download all my data (JSON)", which includes your profile, expenses with receipt items, your share of group expenses, budgets, recurring templates and cards.
  • Secure your account from the Profile screen: change your password, see where you are signed in, and sign out of other devices or all devices.
  • Delete your account and data from the Profile screen ("Delete Account"; you confirm with your password). This permanently deletes your account and personal data as described in Section 6, and cannot be undone. Expenses you added to shared groups remain for the other members with your name removed.
  • Revoke consent (for example, stop using AI features, or opt out of analytics on the website or in the app under Account → Privacy, where crash reports are a separate choice) at any time.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA — including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise any right, contact us at privacy@cerebroos.com.

9. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International data transfers

We operate the Service using infrastructure located in the United States. If you access the Service from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries where we or our providers operate, which may have different data-protection laws than your own.
