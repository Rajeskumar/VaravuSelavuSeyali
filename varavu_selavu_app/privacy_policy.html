TrackSpense Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

TrackSpense is a personal and shared expense tracking product of CerebroOS. This policy covers the TrackSpense web app at expense.cerebroos.com, its iOS and Android apps, and the services that support them. It is separate from the CerebroOS marketing website notice. For privacy questions or requests, email cerebroos@gmail.com.

1. Information we handle

Account and profile. We receive the email address and name you provide, a password hash or Google account information when you choose that sign-in option, and optional profile details such as phone number, mailing address, or payment handles you add. Passwords are stored as password hashes, not readable passwords.

Your financial records. We process expenses, dates, amounts, descriptions, merchant names, categories, budgets, recurring templates, receipt line items, tags, cards you choose to track, income figures you enter, and the insights calculated from these records. We also process groups, member names, invitations, shared expenses, splits, settlements, comments, and balances so members can coordinate shared spending. Other members of a group can see the shared records and member details needed for that group, including optional payment handles you choose to add for settlements; they cannot see your private expenses.

Receipts and AI requests. A receipt image, PDF, or text extracted on your device may be sent to our service for parsing. The app shows a result for your review; it saves expense and receipt line-item data only when you confirm. We also receive questions you ask the AI Analyst and the financial context needed to answer them. Section 4 explains when a third-party AI provider receives any of this information.

Support and feedback. We receive messages and ideas you send through the product. The email service delivers them to our Gmail inbox. Please avoid including account passwords, payment card numbers, or unnecessary sensitive details.

Technical data. Our service and infrastructure may process IP address, browser or device details, app version, request time, authentication and security information, and error information to deliver and protect TrackSpense. If you enable push notifications, we process a device push token and notification content; Expo, Apple, or Google may handle delivery depending on your device.

Local drafts. An unfinished expense can remain on your device for recovery: in browser-tab session storage on web or secure device storage on mobile. It may contain amounts, descriptions, receipt line items, and split choices, but not the receipt photo or sign-in credentials. The draft expires one hour after its last update and is removed on the next access after expiry. Saving, discarding, or deliberately signing out on that device removes it. Local drafts are not included in a server data export.

2. Optional analytics and crash reports

On the web, Google Analytics may measure page and feature usage only if analytics is enabled for the site and you allow it in Analytics preferences. You can revisit that choice in the footer or Account. The analytics tag stays off until you allow it. Sensitive invitation, password-reset, and assistant-approval pages are excluded. Withdrawing permission stops new collection through the app but cannot recall data already sent.

On iOS and Android, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics are separate optional choices in Account → Privacy. If enabled for your app build, usage analytics records which screens and features are used and whether actions worked; crash reports help diagnose failures. Both are off unless you allow them. We do not intentionally include expense values, receipt contents, group details, names, email addresses, sign-in tokens, or AI conversations in these events, and we do not use advertising identifiers or these services for advertising. Google may also receive app, device, and network metadata, including an app-specific identifier and approximate location inferred from IP address. These analytics choices are separate from AI-feature consent and from the CerebroOS marketing website choice.

Google Analytics data is kept according to the settings of the relevant property. Crashlytics data follows Google's retention practices. Because these optional events are not tied to a TrackSpense account identifier, we may be unable to locate or delete a single person's analytics or crash records by account email.

3. Why we use information

We use the information above to provide accounts and the expense tracking service; calculate totals, splits, budgets, and insights; process receipts; answer questions you choose to ask; send requested notifications; respond to support requests; secure and troubleshoot the service; and comply with legal obligations. Where a legal basis is required, our basis for core account and financial processing is providing the service you request. We rely on your choice for optional analytics, crash reporting, and AI features, and on our legitimate interests in security, abuse prevention, and responding to requests where applicable. We do not sell your personal information or share your financial records with advertisers.

4. AI features

TrackSpense asks before using optional AI features. The AI Analyst may send your question and relevant financial context, including expenses, merchants, budgets, and group information where needed, to the model provider selected for that request. The service supports Google Gemini and, when configured, OpenAI or a locally hosted model. The model can return inaccurate or incomplete answers; review them before relying on them.

Receipt parsing first uses text recognition and rules. The mobile app reads receipt text on the device before sending that text to our service. If a receipt read is uncertain and you have allowed AI features, a receipt image or PDF may be sent to Google Gemini for another attempt. Category suggestions may send an expense description to Gemini when built-in rules are insufficient and you have allowed AI features. If you decline, the app can still use its non-AI receipt and category methods.

We do not intentionally send passwords, sign-in tokens, or payment card numbers to the AI provider. TrackSpense does not save AI Analyst chat history as a separate account record; the conversation remains in app memory while you are signed in. A provider may process or retain submitted prompts, files, and responses under its own terms and the configuration used for that request. We have not verified a single retention or model-training rule that covers every provider and configuration, so please avoid putting information in a prompt that is not needed for your request. You can stop future AI requests by withdrawing the app's AI choice or not using the feature. This does not recall a request already sent.

5. Sharing and connected services

We share information needed to operate TrackSpense with hosting and security providers, currently Google Cloud and Cloudflare; Gmail for support and transactional email; Google for optional sign-in, analytics, crash reporting, and Gemini processing when those features are used; an OpenAI model service if selected and configured; and Expo, Apple, or Google for push delivery when enabled. Those providers may process technical information needed to deliver their services. We may also disclose information where required by law or necessary to protect users and the service.

If you join a shared group, other members can see that group's records and balances, including expenses, splits, settlements, and any names or details entered into those records. If you choose to connect an external assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT, the assistant receives the personal-expense data you authorize in its permissions. Its provider handles that data under its own policy. Group expenses are excluded from this connection. Preparing an expense through an assistant does not save it without your separate review in TrackSpense. You can disconnect an assistant to stop future access, but doing so cannot erase data the external provider has already received.

6. Retention and account deletion

We keep account and financial records while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. When an account deletion request succeeds, the account and private expenses are removed from the active database. Shared group expenses and related records remain for other members' balances and history; your group member name is changed to “Anonymous User” and your account email is removed from the group membership. Text you entered into shared descriptions, notes, or comments may still be visible to other members. Some records may remain where required for legal obligations or dispute resolution. Backup copies age out under our provider's backup schedule; we have not confirmed a specific backup retention period.

We aim to delete product support and feedback emails from our Gmail inbox within 12 months, unless a specific request, dispute, or legal obligation requires longer. Local expense drafts expire as described in Section 1. Connected-assistant drafts expire after 24 hours; detailed results are pruned after 30 days, activity after 180 days, and rate-limit windows after two days, subject to the cleanup schedule. Minimal identifiers may be kept to prevent duplicate expense creation. Analytics and AI providers apply their own retention practices as described above.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and update profile and expense information in TrackSpense, export your expenses as CSV or download your account data as JSON, and delete your account from the Profile screen. Password-based accounts confirm deletion with a password; accounts without a usable password confirm with their email address. Account deletion cannot be undone, subject to the shared-record and backup limits above. You can change web analytics preferences, mobile analytics and crash-report choices, and AI-feature choices in the app. You can disconnect a connected assistant, sign out of other devices, or change your password where available.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, and to complain to a privacy authority. For requests or questions, email cerebroos@gmail.com. Include enough information to locate your account or message without sending unnecessary sensitive details. We will respond according to applicable law.

8. Security and international processing

We use measures such as encrypted connections and access controls to protect information. No service can guarantee absolute security. TrackSpense is operated from the United States, and data may be processed in the United States and other countries where our providers operate. Their data-protection rules may differ from those where you live.

9. Children, changes, and contact

TrackSpense is intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect account data from children. If you believe a child has used the service, contact us at cerebroos@gmail.com.

We may update this policy when the product or its practices change. We will post the new version with an updated date and give additional notice when applicable law requires it. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to cerebroos@gmail.com.
