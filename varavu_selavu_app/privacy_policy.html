This Privacy Policy explains how CerebroOS ("CerebroOS," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use TrackSpense, our expense-tracking application, available on the web at expense.cerebroos.com and as a mobile app (together, the "Service"). By using the Service you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who we are

The Service is operated by CerebroOS, located at CerebroOS HQ. For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at privacy@cerebroos.com.

2. Information we collect

a. Information you provide

Category Examples Why we collect it
Account & profile Email address, name, phone number, and (optionally) mailing address To create and secure your account and provide the Service
Financial & expense data Expense descriptions, merchant/store names, amounts, dates, categories, recurring-payment templates, and income figures you enter Core functionality — to record, categorize, and analyze your spending
Receipts & uploads Receipt images you upload and the text extracted from them ("Parse Receipt" OCR). Receipt images may be read by a third-party AI service (see Section 4). To let you capture expenses from receipts
AI queries Questions you type into the AI Analyst and the financial context needed to answer them To generate answers about your spending
Support & ideas Messages, feedback, and ideas you submit To respond to you and improve the Service

Unsaved expense drafts. To recover interrupted entries, the web app keeps a draft in this browser tab's session storage, and the mobile app uses the device's secure storage. This can include amounts, descriptions, receipt line items and split choices, but not receipt photos or sign-in credentials. Drafts are available for recovery for one hour after the last draft update; expired drafts are removed on the next access. Saving, choosing "Discard draft", or explicitly signing out on that client removes its draft. Signing in as a different account also removes the previous draft. Unsaved drafts are local to the device and are not included in the server data export. Closing Ask keeps its conversation in app memory while you are signed in; signing out clears it.

b. Information collected automatically

When you use the Service we automatically collect certain technical and usage data, including device and browser type, IP address, pages viewed, and interactions. We use Google Analytics for this. On the web, our infrastructure provider (Cloudflare) may also process request data to deliver and protect the Service.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to: provide, maintain, and improve the Service; record and analyze your expenses; generate insights, charts, and AI-assisted answers; process recurring-expense templates; authenticate you and secure your account; respond to support requests; monitor performance and prevent abuse; and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Artificial intelligence features

Three features send data to a third-party AI service, currently Google's Gemini:

  • Ask (the AI Analyst). Your question and the figures needed to answer it: expense descriptions, merchant names, amounts and dates, budgets, and for groups you belong to, the group name, member display names and balances.
  • Receipt scanning. Receipts are read by our own text recognition first. Only when that read isn't confident is the receipt image or PDF sent to the AI service, and never if you haven't allowed AI features.
  • Category suggestions. The description text of an expense, only when our built-in rules can't categorize it.

We do not send your password, payment card numbers, or access tokens. The first time you use one of these features the app asks whether you allow it; if you decline, nothing is sent and the rest of the app keeps working (receipts are read by our own reader only, and category suggestions use only built-in rules). You can withdraw the choice at any time by clearing the app's stored data, and we may ask again after material changes. We send only what is needed to answer the request. The provider handles that data under its own terms for the Gemini API [CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLISHING: the provider's retention period and whether data is used to train models for the API plan in use]. Prompts and answers are not stored by TrackSpense beyond the conversation on your screen.

5. How we share information

We share personal information only as follows:

  • Service providers. Vendors who process data on our behalf to run the Service, including hosting (Google Cloud), content delivery/security (Cloudflare), analytics (Google Analytics, only if you accept analytics cookies), the AI service (Google Gemini, only if you allow AI features), and Google sign-in (loaded only when you choose "Continue with Google"). They are permitted to use the data only to provide services to us and must protect it to a standard equivalent to this Policy.
  • Legal & safety. When required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users, the public, or CerebroOS.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share your financial data with advertisers.

6. Data retention

We retain your personal and financial information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. When you delete your account (see Section 8), your profile, personal expenses and receipt items, budgets, recurring templates, tags, cards, sign-ins and invitation records are deleted immediately.

Shared group records stay. Expenses you added to a group, with their descriptions, merchants, notes and amounts, remain so the other members' balances and history stay correct. Your name on those records becomes "Anonymous User" and your email address is removed from them. Copies in system backups age out on the backup schedule of our hosting provider [CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLISHING: backup retention period]. We may also keep certain records where we must to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you as required by law.

8. Your rights and choices

You can:

  • Access and update your profile and expense data within the Service.
  • Export your data from the Profile screen: "Export all expenses (CSV)" or "Download all my data (JSON)", which includes your profile, expenses with receipt items, your share of group expenses, budgets, recurring templates and cards.
  • Secure your account from the Profile screen: change your password, see where you are signed in, and sign out of other devices or all devices.
  • Delete your account and data from the Profile screen ("Delete Account"; you confirm with your password). This permanently deletes your account and personal data as described in Section 6, and cannot be undone. Expenses you added to shared groups remain for the other members with your name removed.
  • Revoke consent (for example, stop using AI features or opt out of analytics) at any time.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA — including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise any right, contact us at privacy@cerebroos.com.

9. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International data transfers

We operate the Service using infrastructure located in the United States. If you access the Service from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries where we or our providers operate, which may have different data-protection laws than your own.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, notify you in the Service. Your continued use after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.
